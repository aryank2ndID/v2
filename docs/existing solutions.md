Ashva WearTech (Bengaluru/Hyderabad) — "Fitknees" + "Fitmust" - link {https://ashvaweartech.com/}

Founded 2019, BIRAC-funded, TRL 7/8 (near-market-ready, not a lab prototype).
4 wireless IMU-style motion sensors strapped to thigh and shin — same placement as your ESP32 kit.
Bluetooth-connected to a clinician's tablet; ~20–30 min assessment; measures range of motion, muscle strength, balance, proprioception, gait — outputs an AI-scored report called a "Kneeport."
Explicitly marketed as "a blood test for your knees" — decides conservative-treatment-vs-surgery.
Already ran rural farmer knee-screening camps near Bengaluru at subsidized rates, in partnership with rural hospitals — i.e., they have already piloted the exact rural-outreach use case SIH26004 is asking for.
Patented (UK + Hong Kong granted; India/US pending). B2B model — sold to ~100 physiotherapy/ortho clinics across 6 cities, ~$58K seed funding on record.
