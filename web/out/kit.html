IMU frame layout

20 bytes — fits the default BLE 4.2 ATT payload, so no MTU negotiation is needed
ByteFieldEncoding
0type | sensor idupper nibble type, lower nibble sensor
1sequenceuint8, wraps — gaps are dropped frames
2–3device timestampuint16 LE, ms, wraps every 65.5 s
4–9accel x, y, zint16 LE · ±4 g · 8192 LSB/g
10–15gyro x, y, zint16 LE · ±2000 dps · 16.384 LSB/dps
16–17flagsbit0 saturation · bit1 cuff slip · bit2 low battery
18–19CRC-16CCITT-FALSE over bytes 0–17, big endian
Two IMUs at 100 Hz is 200 frames/s, 4 kB/s. That fits a 15 ms connection interval on a five-year-old Android with room to spare.
