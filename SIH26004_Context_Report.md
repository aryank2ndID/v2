## 1. Knee Osteoarthritis Prevalence in India

### Key Figures

| Metric | Value | Source |
|--------|-------|--------|
| Overall knee OA prevalence (community survey, n=5000, radiographic) | **28.7%** | Pal et al. 2016 |
| Systematic review & meta-analysis pooled prevalence (16 studies, India) | **20.24%** (95% CI: 11.90–30.13) | Hazra et al. 2025 |
| Total OA prevalence, age-standardized (India, GBD 2019) | **5,313 per 100,000** (95% UI: 4,799–5,898) | Singh et al. 2022 (GBD 2019) |
| Total persons with OA in India (GBD 2019) | **62.35 million** (up from 23.46M in 1990) | Singh et al. 2022 |
| OA as cause of YLDs in India (2019) | **20th most common** (1.48% of all YLDs) | Singh et al. 2022 |
| Knee OA — most common site | **Yes** — most prevalent form of OA in India | Singh et al. 2022; Pal et al. 2016 |

### Detailed Sources

1. **Pal CP et al. (2016)** — "Epidemiology of knee osteoarthritis in India and related factors." *Indian J Orthop* 50(5):518–522.
   - Community-based cross-sectional study across 5 Indian sites (Dehradun/Nainital, Agra, Pune/Pimpri, Bengaluru/Hyderabad, Kolkata). N=5,000 subjects ≥40 years.
   - Overall prevalence: **28.7%**; female prevalence 31.6% vs male 28.1% (P=0.007).
   - Village prevalence: 31.1%; big city: 33.1%.
   - **Cited by 643+** — highly defensible landmark study.
   - DOI: 10.4103/0019-5413.189608 | PMID: 27746495
   - URL: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC5017174

2. **Hazra S et al. (2025)** — "Prevalence of Knee Osteoarthritis in India: A Systematic Review and Meta-Analysis of Population-Based Studies." *Springer Nature*.
   - Searched PubMed, Ovid, Scopus through Dec 2023.
   - Pooled prevalence: **20.24%** (95% CI: 11.90–30.13) across 16 moderate-quality heterogeneous studies.
   - Most prevalent risk factors: advancing age, female gender, lower socioeconomic strata, lesser education, previous knee injury.
   - URL: https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s43465-025-01520-4

3. **Daniel RA et al. (2025)** — "Prevalence of knee osteoarthritis among elderly persons in India: A systematic review and meta-analysis."
   - PMCID: PMC12178484 | DOI: 10.4103/jfmpc.jfmpc_1254_24 | PMID: 40547750
   - Focuses specifically on elderly (60+) population.
   - URL: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/40547750

4. **Yadav R et al. (2022)** — "Prevalence of Primary Knee Osteoarthritis in the Urban and Rural Population in India." *Indian J Rheumatol* 17(3):239–243.
   - Confirming Pal et al.'s 28.7% figure; breakdown by city/village/town.
   - DOI: 10.4103/injr.injr_337_20

> **Most defensible numbers:** The **28.7%** (Pal et al. 2016, n=5000, widely cited 643+ times) and the meta-analytic **20.24%** (Hazra et al. 2025) are the strongest citable figures for national-level knee OA prevalence in India.

## 2. Prevalence & Burden in the North Eastern Region (NER)

### Key Figures

| Metric | Value | Source |
|--------|-------|--------|
| Knee OA prevalence — tea garden community, Jorhat, Assam | **29.4%** (94/320) | Buragohain et al. 2023 |
| Female prevalence in tea garden study | **33.5%** (vs 22.8% male) | Buragohain et al. 2023 |
| NER total population | **~46 million** (~3.7% of India) | NEIGRIHMS; Census 2011+projections |
| NER doctor-to-population ratio | **~1:5,000** (vs national 1:2,000 at time of report) | NEIGRIHMS |
| NER overall health manpower deficiency | **~50% medical manpower; ~60% health sector** | NEIGRIHMS |

### Detailed Sources

5. **Buragohain CR et al. (2023)** — "Prevalence of primary osteoarthritis of knee in tea garden community of Jorhat District, Assam." *IP Int J Orthop Rheumatol* 9(1):25–29.
   - Community-based cross-sectional study, 320 adults ≥40 years in 5 tea gardens, Jorhat district.
   - Overall prevalence: **29.4%**. Higher in females (33.5%) than males (22.8%). Higher in age >50 years (OR 4.53, 95% CI 2.54–8.06).
   - Risk factors identified: prolonged standing, squatting, weight lifting, walking on uneven/sloping terrain.
   - DOI: 10.18231/j.ijor.2023.004
   - URL: https://ijor.org/archive/volume/9/issue/1/article/7334

6. **NEIGRIHMS Official Description** (North Eastern Indira Gandhi Regional Institute of Health and Medical Sciences).
   - NER population ~46 million, ~3.7% of Indian population.
   - Area ~2.6 lakh sq km, ~400 hospitals.
   - Doctor-to-population ratio ~**1:5,000** vs national ~1:2,000.
   - 50% deficiency in medical manpower; 60% overall in health sector.
   - URL: https://neigrihms.gov.in/readmore_Eng.html

7. **GBD 2019 State-wise India Burden** (Singh et al. 2022).
   - State-level data available: Assam, Manipur, etc. are among the states modelled.
   - Goa (5,689/100K), Rajasthan (5,667/100K), Kerala (5,658/100K) had highest age-standardized OA prevalence.
   - NER states not individually among the top-3 but terrain + occupational risk factors compound.
   - DOI: 10.1016/j.joca.2022.05.004

### Note on NER-specific risk factors
The NER context features: hilly/mountainous terrain; prolonged squatting (paddy cultivation); tea-garden harvesting with repetitive knee-loading; stairs in traditional housing; limited orthopaedic access. While no large NER-wide OA prevalence study exists, the Jorhat tea-garden study (29.4%) and the national survey data (28.7% in villages) strongly suggest comparable or higher rates.

    - DOI: 10.1016/j.joca.2022.05.004 | PMID: 35598766

    - Radiological knee OA more common (28.7%) than symptomatic (12.5%).
    - URL: https://www.thelancet.com/journals/eclinm/article/PIIS2589-5370(20)30331X/fulltext

> **Most defensible numbers:** The GBD 2021 Lancet paper (595M, projected 1B by 2050) is the single most authoritative global estimate. For India specifically, Singh et al. 2022 (62.35M, GBD 2019) is the standard reference.

## 4. Economic Cost of Knee OA & Joint Replacement Surgery in India

### Key Figures

| Metric | Value | Source |
|--------|-------|--------|
| Total knee replacement (TKR) cost — India | **₹1,50,000 – ₹4,50,000 per knee** | Multiple hospital/costing sources |
| TKR cost in India (PPP-adjusted, cheapest globally) | **$3,457** (INR) | PMC systematic review 2023 |
| TKR cost — USA (comparison) | **$19,568** (PPP) | PMC systematic review 2023 |
| Bilateral TKR cost — India | **₹3,50,000 – ₹8,00,000** | Surya Hospitals 2025 |
| TKR surgeries performed annually in India | **~150,000–250,000** | PMC (2024); Aakash Healthcare |
| Budget impact (40% TKR coverage scenario) | **₹5,013 crore (2023)** to **₹8,444 crore (2028)** | Pharmacoecon Open 2024 |
| ICER for TKR (age 50, India) | **₹36,107/QALY** (USD 482.90) — cost-effective | Pharmacoecon Open 2024 |
| India total health expenditure (2021-22) | **3.83% of GDP**; per capita ₹6,602 | NSS 80th Round 2026; NHA |
| OOP share of total health expenditure | **47.1%** (2019-20) | Frontiers Public Health 2025 |
| Average OOP per hospitalization (India) | **₹34,064** (rural ₹31,484; urban ₹38,688) | NSS 80th Round, Jan-Dec 2025 |
| Catastrophic health expenditure (CHE) incidence | **~30%** at 10% threshold (meta-analysis, India) | Springer 2024 |
| NCD-related CHE (NSS data) | **50%** at 10% threshold; **25%** at 30% threshold | Value in Health Regional Issues 2021 |

### Detailed Sources

12. **Kamath KR et al. (2024)** — "Economic Evaluation of Total Knee Replacement Compared with Non-Surgical Management for Knee Osteoarthritis in India." *Pharmacoecon Open* 9(2):217–229.
    - ~150,000 TKRs/year in India currently.
    - ICER ranges: ₹36,107/QALY (age 50) to ₹61,363/QALY (age 70) — cost-effective at WTP threshold ₹140,000.
    - DOI: 10.1007/s41669-024-00541-3 | PMC11865410
    - URL: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC11865410

13. **Systematic Review — Costs of TKR (2023)** — "Costs and models used in the economic analysis of Total Knee Replacement (TKR)." *BMC/PMC*.
    - India: **$3,457** (cheapest globally). USA: $19,568. Average PPP: $15,616.
    - DOI: PMC10368258
    - URL: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC10368258/

14. **NSS 80th Round Health Survey (Jan–Dec 2025)**, released April 2026.
    - Average OOP per hospitalization: ₹34,064 (rural ₹31,484).
    - Health insurance coverage: 47.4% rural, 44.3% urban (up from 14.1%/19.1% in 2017-18).
    - URL: https://mospi.gov.in/uploads/latestReleases/latest_release_1776680784456_860ea057-f170-4d41-a1ae-48bb69faf10c_PRESS_NOTE_HEALTH_REVISED.pdf

15. **Pharmacoeconomics of TKR in India** — "Is It Worth Delaying Total Knee Replacement as Late as Possible?" *Value in Health Regional Issues* (2021).
    - TKR cost-effective if payer willing to pay ≥₹9,789/QALY.
    - Delaying TKR decreases QALYs and increases ICER.
    - DOI: https://doi.org/10.1016/j.vhri.2021.04.005

16. **OOP & CHE in India** — "Understanding out-of-pocket expenditure in India: a systematic review." *Frontiers Public Health* 13 (2025).
    - OOP = 47.1% of total health expenditure (2019-20).
    - Total health expenditure per capita: ₹6,602 (2021-22).
    - DOI: 10.3389/fpubh.2025.1594542

> **Most defensible numbers:** TKR at **₹1.5–4.5 lakh per knee** (widely corroborated); India has the **cheapest TKR globally at ~$3,457 PPP**; OOP health spending at **47.1%** of total health expenditure is the latest NHA figure.

## Summary: Why This Problem Matters — Key Take-Need Statement

| Dimension | Evidence |
|-----------|----------|
| **Scale of disease** | 62.35 million Indians with OA (2019); projected ~1 billion globally by 2050 |
| **NER-specific burden** | 29.4% prevalence in tea gardens (Assam); hilly terrain + repetitive knee loading |
| **Demographic driver** | 173M elderly by 2026, 230M by 2036, 319M by 2050 — OA prevalence scales with age |
| **Healthcare access gap** | NER doctor ratio 1:5,000 (vs national 1:811); 80% specialist shortfall at rural CHCs |
| **Economic burden** | TKR costs ₹1.5–4.5 lakh/knee; 47% health spending is OOP; 30% households face catastrophic health expenditure |
| **Enabling infrastructure** | 10.29 lakh ASHAs already doing NCD screening; 96%+ villages have 4G; 85.5% households own smartphones |
| **Feasibility of offline-first** | Rural internet access 83.3%; smartphone penetration high; ASHA digital tools already deployed under Ayushman Bharat |
