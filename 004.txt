  # SIH26004 — What exactly is the problem?                                     
                                                                                
  **Problem:** *AI-Assisted Early Detection System for Osteoarthritis (OA) Risk 
  Markers in North Eastern Region (NER)*                                        
                                                                                
  **Organization:** Ministry of Development of North Eastern Region (MDoNER)    
  **Category:** **Hardware**                                                    
  **Theme:** Space Technology                                                   
  **Submission deadline:** **20 September 2026**                                
  **Maximum listed ideas:** 500 ([SIH 2026 Problem Statements][1])              
                                                                                
  The important thing is that although this involves AI/software, **SIH         
  classifies it as a Hardware PS**. So your solution should not be just a web   
  application. You need a **physical/portable assessment component** as part of 
  the prototype. ([GitHub][2])                                                  
                                                                                
  ---                                                                           
                                                                                
  # 1. What is MDoNER actually asking you to build?                             
                                                                                
  In simple terms:                                                              
                                                                                
  > **Build a portable system that can be taken to a rural/remote healthcare    
  camp, examine a person's movement/joint-related indicators, use AI to         
  estimate their risk of Osteoarthritis, and give a healthcare worker a         
  preliminary screening report — without requiring reliable Internet.**         
                                                                                
  The target users are **healthcare workers**, not necessarily orthopedic       
  specialists.                                                                  
                                                                                
  The system should help identify people who **may be at risk of OA early**, so 
  that they can be referred for proper medical evaluation.                      
                                                                                
  This is particularly important in the North Eastern Region because remote     
  terrain, physically demanding occupations, aging populations and limited      
  access to specialist orthopedic care make conventional screening difficult.   
  ([SIH 2026 Problem Statements][1])                                            
                                                                                
  ---                                                                           
                                                                                
  # 2. What inputs does the system need?                                        
                                                                                
  The PS explicitly identifies several possible sources of information:         
                                                                                
  ### A. Joint movement analysis                                                
                                                                                
  You need to examine how a joint moves.                                        
                                                                                
  For example:                                                                  
                                                                                
  * Knee flexion/extension                                                      
  * Range of motion                                                             
  * Movement symmetry                                                           
  * Movement speed                                                              
  * Abnormal movement patterns                                                  
  * Left/right differences                                                      
                                                                                
  This could potentially be measured using:                                     
                                                                                
  * IMUs                                                                        
  * Accelerometers                                                              
  * Gyroscopes                                                                  
  * Smartphone sensors                                                          
  * Camera/computer vision                                                      
  * Wearable sensors                                                            
                                                                                
  ---                                                                           
                                                                                
  ### B. Gait analysis                                                          
                                                                                
  The system should potentially analyze **how someone walks**.                  
                                                                                
  Possible parameters:                                                          
                                                                                
  * Walking speed                                                               
  * Step length                                                                 
  * Cadence                                                                     
  * Stride symmetry                                                             
  * Stance time                                                                 
  * Swing time                                                                  
  * Knee movement                                                               
  * Hip movement                                                                
  * Foot placement                                                              
  * Left/right asymmetry                                                        
                                                                                
  A camera-based approach is possible, but the PS also explicitly allows        
  **sensor-based assessment**. ([SIH 2026 Problem Statements][1])
